Outcomes

  • Apply discrete-mathematics principles in applied settings.
  • Reason mathematically about discrete structures and data types.
  • Understand and synthesize elementary proof.

Study Tips

  • Mile wide, foot deep — do not fall behind.
  • Ask questions; be active.
  • Practice proofs consistently; precision matters.

Recommended Books

Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications

Kenneth H. Rosen — 7th Edition, McGraw-Hill (or any other edition). The primary textbook for this course.

Invitation to Discrete Mathematics

Jiří Matoušek & Jaroslav Nešetřil. Optional reading.

Discrete Mathematics: Elementary and Beyond

László Lovász & József Pelikán. Optional reading.
